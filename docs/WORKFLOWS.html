How each role moves through the system

Eleven roles share one REST API and one database. What separates them is not a different application — it is the permission set checked on every request and the data scope applied inside every query. This document traces the working day of each role: where they land, what they touch, where their work is handed to someone else, and where the system refuses them.

One gate, four scopes

Every role's day starts at the same place and narrows at the same two checkpoints. The API checks permissions, never role names. Services then trim the result set to the role's scope. A record outside scope returns 404, not 403, so its existence is never disclosed.

Only this role can

Refused even here

Also the Principal's alone

  • Emergency broadcast — bypasses quiet hours and per-user notification settings, cannot be recalled.
  • An Emergency, Lockdown or Evacuation broadcast auto-opens a Critical incident owned by the sender.
  • Early Warning review — accept opens an intervention with a named owner; dismiss suppresses that cause for 30 days.

Refused

  • User administration and system settings — those belong to the two admin roles.
  • Approving a payroll run they calculated themselves.

Where the AI Co-Pilot fits

  • Reads only your classes: objectives, approved question bank, results, attendance, homework.
  • Produces a draft — lesson, worksheet, quiz, report comments, parent message, weekly brief.
  • Nothing is sent automatically. A named teacher approves before anything is published.

Class scope in practice

  • No Command Center and no attendance analytics — both need school-wide student read.
  • A student outside your sections returns 404, so you cannot tell whether they exist.
  • Read-only on profiles: no edits, no medical or counselling notes.

Their own HR record

  • A teacher is an employee too: Staff Self-Service shows their own roster, attendance, leave balance, overtime and payslips.
  • Self-scoped like every other holder — the /me/… routes resolve the signed-in employee and take no id, so one teacher can never read another's record.
  • Leave still goes to HR for approval, and the payslip appears only once payroll is released.

HR

workforce + payroll prepschool scope

HR owns the employee record from joining to payslip, and approves leave and overtime. It prepares payroll but deliberately cannot approve it — that permission sits with the Principal, and a second rule stops whoever calculated a run from approving it even if they hold both.

Approving leave does four things

  • Consumes the employee's leave balance.
  • Writes staff attendance to On Leave across the range, clearing any check-in.
  • Flips overlapping roster lines to Cover needed.
  • Notifies the requester — the response names how many roster lines now need cover.

Guard rails on submission

  • Leave: not more than 30 days retrospective, not more than 180 ahead, Sundays excluded, balance checked.
  • Overtime: no future dates, 60 days old at most, 16 hours a day at most, half-hour steps.

Front desk, same role

  • Visitors: Expected → Inside → Completed, or Denied. The host is notified on arrival.
  • Pickup: a new person is added as Pending, verified by OTP, then Verified before a first collection.
  • An unauthorised pickup attempt holds the collection and alerts the Principal and the office.
  • Documents a parent uploads land here for verification.

Prepares, never approves

  • Office can raise, submit and pay an expense but cannot approve one — that needs Finance.
  • Office requests a certificate; the decision chain ends with the Principal.
  • Student tracking shows current position only, no history.

Non-Teaching Staff

self-service + dutyself scope

A split role, and the split is the point. For anything about themselves — shifts, leave, payslips — the scope is self and there is no way to address another employee: every endpoint is /me/… with no id to change. For gate and transport duty the scope is campus-wide.

Who else has self-service. The selfservice.use permission is held by Non-Teaching Staff, Teacher and Super Admin (the last through its wildcard grant). School Admin is explicitly denied it, along with the four portal permissions. HR, Finance, Office, Principal and Management have no self-service screen — they reach leave and attendance through the HR screens instead, where the permission is hr.read and the scope is the whole school. Anyone whose account is not linked to an employee record sees an empty state rather than another person's data.

Parent

3 permissions onlyfamily scope

The smallest permission set that still does a lot: portal, own children's tracking, own fees. Every request is checked twice — the parent id must be your own, and the child must be linked to you. Tracking needs a third check, a per-child consent flag, and history stops at seven days.
